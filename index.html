Why this isn't the game cheating you

Every pull is independent — the game has no memory of your last 166 misses. A drought feels wrong because your brain expects the average (166 pulls) to be roughly what actually happens every time. It isn't: with a 0.6% rate, some players get the rare drop on pull 3, and a meaningful slice of players are still empty-handed past pull 400 — both are exactly what the same fair coin produces. The histogram above is that population. If your marker sits deep in the tail, you're not unlucky in a way that needs explaining; you're a normal, expected outcome of the distribution.

One thing this demo deliberately leaves out: most real gacha games add a "pity" system that guarantees the rare drop after a fixed number of tries. That's a second mechanic layered on top of the probability — this page shows what the probability alone feels like, without that safety net.
